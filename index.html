About me

I started out as a graphic designer, learned to code so I could stop handing off static mockups, and never went back. Today I sit between design and engineering — comfortable in Figma, comfortable in a terminal, happiest shipping.

I care about semantic markup, keyboard-first interaction, and interfaces that degrade gracefully on a slow connection. Currently based in Lisbon, working with teams in CET and EST.
